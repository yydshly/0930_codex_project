# 海岬果岭素材

Authored course, ball, club, flag, ramp, wooden borders and miniature structures: [Kenney Minigolf Kit 3.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/minigolf-kit), CC0. The original `License.txt` and required `Textures/colormap.png` ship beside the selected models.

Decorative palm trees, rocks and shrubs reuse the existing [Kenney Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit), CC0; its original license remains in `web/assets/showcase/nature/License.txt`.

Rigid-body physics reuse the pinned [cannon-es 0.20.0](https://github.com/pmndrs/cannon-es) module, MIT, with full license at `web/vendor/physics/LICENSE`.

The original archive, SHA256, all selected models and physics derivation are recorded in `assets/game-forms/motion-putt-sources.json`.
